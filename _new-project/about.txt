name: Landing Page Design, Deployment & Domain Management for Dr. Sara Ragab
Project Overview

Dr. Sara Ragab needed a professional landing page for her Saudi AI educational initiative. The goal wasn't simply to build a webpage—it was to create a clean, trustworthy experience that could convert visitors into WhatsApp inquiries while remaining easy to expand as the business grows.

The client already owned a domain and wanted a minimalist design focused on elegance rather than unnecessary sections. The landing page needed to communicate the project's value clearly through a compelling headline, key benefits, testimonials, and a direct WhatsApp call-to-action.

The Challenge

Beyond the visual design, there were several technical considerations:

Deploy the project without disrupting the existing website.
Configure the domain to support multiple future projects.
Keep hosting costs as low as possible.
Build an infrastructure that could easily scale to different regional landing pages in the future.
Deliver a fast-loading, mobile-first experience suitable for advertising campaigns.
My Solution

I designed and developed a responsive landing page with a clean visual hierarchy and a conversion-focused layout.

The project also included:

Responsive UI for desktop and mobile.
WhatsApp integration.
Domain and DNS configuration.
Deployment and hosting management.
Future-ready subdomain architecture (such as sa.domain.com and eg.domain.com).
Technical consultation on domain strategy and long-term scalability.
14-day post-delivery support for any implementation issues.

Rather than treating the project as a one-page website, I advised the client on building a flexible brand structure that could support multiple markets and future landing pages without purchasing separate domains.
